# Transfers

## How a transfer works

When you send money, ITL Pay first reserves the amount in your account, then screens the transfer for fraud, then completes it. You see the status change in the app. Most transfers complete within 30 seconds.

## Transfer statuses

- **PENDING**: we have your request and have reserved the money. Nothing has left your account yet.
- **COMPLETED**: the money has reached the recipient's account.
- **FAILED**: the transfer was stopped, for example by fraud screening, and the reserved money was returned to your available balance. A failed transfer is never charged a fee.
- **REJECTED**: the transfer was refused before anything was reserved, usually because the available balance was too low.

## Checking a transfer

Every transfer has a reference that starts with TRF, for example TRF-1. You can ask the assistant for the status of a transfer by its reference, or open Transfers in the app. You can only see transfers from your own accounts.

## Transfers that stay pending

If a transfer is still PENDING after 30 minutes, do not send it again: a second transfer with a new reference would be a second payment. Contact support with the reference; we will trace it.
