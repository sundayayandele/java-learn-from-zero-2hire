# Fees and limits

## Transfer fees

Transfers between ITL Pay accounts are free. Transfers to other Nigerian banks cost ₦10 for amounts up to ₦5,000, ₦25 for amounts from ₦5,001 to ₦50,000, and ₦50 above ₦50,000. Stamp duty of ₦50 applies to transfers of ₦10,000 or more into an account, as required by law.

## Daily limits by account tier

- **Tier 1** (phone number and BVN or NIN): you can send up to ₦50,000 a day and hold at most ₦300,000.
- **Tier 2** (Tier 1 plus a verified ID document): up to ₦200,000 a day and a maximum balance of ₦500,000.
- **Tier 3** (Tier 2 plus a verified address): up to ₦5,000,000 a day with no maximum balance.

Limits reset at midnight, Lagos time. To move to a higher tier, open Profile, then Upgrade account.

## Fraud screening on large transfers

Transfers above ₦20,000 get extra fraud screening. Most clear within a minute; if one is stopped, its status becomes FAILED and the money returns to your available balance.
