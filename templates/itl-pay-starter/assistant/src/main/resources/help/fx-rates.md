# Foreign exchange

## Rates

ITL Pay shows live buy and sell rates for USD-NGN, GBP-NGN and EUR-NGN. The rate you see in the stream changes every few seconds.

## Quotes

To convert money, ask for a quote. A quote fixes the rate for 30 seconds. If you confirm within 30 seconds you get that rate; after that, ask for a new quote. We never change a confirmed quote.

## FX fees

There is no separate FX fee: our margin is included in the difference between the buy and the sell rate.
