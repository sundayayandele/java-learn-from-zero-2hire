# Reversals, refunds and disputes

## Money left my account but the recipient did not get it

If the status is COMPLETED but the recipient says they have not received the money, ask them to check again after 24 hours: some banks credit late. If they still have nothing, open a dispute from the transfer in the app. We investigate within 3 working days and, where the money did not arrive, reverse it within 5 working days in total.

## I sent money to the wrong account

Open a dispute from the transfer as soon as possible. We will contact the receiving bank, but we cannot reverse a completed transfer without the recipient's consent or a court order, so the outcome is not guaranteed.

## Failed transfers

When a transfer FAILS, the reserved amount returns to your available balance immediately. No refund request is needed and no fee is charged.

## Complaints

If you are not happy with how we handled a dispute, you can escalate to our complaints team at complaints@itlpay.example. We reply within 2 working days. If we cannot resolve it, you can refer the complaint to the Central Bank of Nigeria's Consumer Protection Department.
