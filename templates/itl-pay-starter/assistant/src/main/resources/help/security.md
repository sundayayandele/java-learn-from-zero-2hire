# Keeping your account safe

## PIN, password and one-time codes

ITL Pay staff and the ITL Pay assistant will never ask for your PIN, password or one-time code (OTP). Never share them with anyone, including someone who says they work for ITL Pay. If you shared one, change your PIN in the app straight away and contact support.

## Lost phone or suspicious activity

If you lose your phone or see a transaction you did not make, freeze your account from the website or call support on 0700-ITL-PAY-1. Freezing stops all outgoing transfers until you unfreeze it.

## What the assistant can do

The assistant can answer questions about ITL Pay, show the balance of your own accounts, check the status of your own transfers and show exchange rates. It cannot send money, change your details or reverse a transfer. For those, use the app or contact support.
