# ECB sample data

`eurofxref-hist-sample.csv` contains five unmodified rows (16, 19 and 20 October 2026; 4 and 5 January 1999) of the ECB's euro foreign exchange reference rates history file, downloaded from <https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/eurofxref/eurofxref-hist.zip> on 22 October 2026.

Source: ECB statistics. Reused under the [ESCB policy on reuse of statistics](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/ecb_statistics/governance_and_quality_framework/html/usage_policy.en.html).
