Every dataset used by the capstones is **free for both commercial and non-commercial use**. Licences were checked at the source on **22 October 2026**; recheck before you publish anything built on them, because terms can change.

**Always:** keep the attribution line in your README, don't present derived figures as official statistics, and don't commit large raw files to Git (download them with a script; commit only small samples).

## Details and download instructions

### ECB euro reference rates

- **What:** daily reference rates for about 30 currencies against the euro, published around 16:00 CET on TARGET working days, history from 1999.
- **Download:** <https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/eurofxref/eurofxref-hist.zip> (CSV, all history) and <https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/eurofxref/eurofxref-daily.xml> (latest day). Overview: <https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/policy_and_exchange_rates/euro_reference_exchange_rates/html/index.en.html>
- **Terms:** [ESCB policy on reuse of statistics](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/ecb_statistics/governance_and_quality_framework/html/usage_policy.en.html): free reuse for commercial and non-commercial purposes, provided the source is quoted and the statistics are not modified. Cross rates you compute (for example NGN/USD via EUR) are *your derived figures*: label them as such.
- **Note:** the ECB does not publish an NGN rate. Use the US Treasury dataset below for NGN, or treat NGN as a configurable "house rate".

### US Treasury Reporting Rates of Exchange

- **What:** quarterly exchange rates for about 170 currencies (including the Nigerian naira) used by US government agencies.
- **Download/API:** <https://fiscaldata.treasury.gov/datasets/treasury-reporting-rates-exchange/treasury-reporting-rates-of-exchange>; API endpoint `https://api.fiscaldata.treasury.gov/services/api/fiscal_service/v1/accounting/od/rates_of_exchange`
- **Terms:** [Fiscal Data licensing](https://fiscaldata.treasury.gov/about-us/): "available to copy, adapt, redistribute, or otherwise use for non-commercial and commercial purposes."

### World Bank WDI

- **What:** indicator `FR.INR.LEND`, lending interest rate (%), by country and year (Nigeria: `NGA`).
- **API:** <https://api.worldbank.org/v2/country/NGA/indicator/FR.INR.LEND?format=json>
- **Terms:** [World Bank data licences](https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/public-licenses): CC BY 4.0. Attribution: "Source: World Bank, World Development Indicators (CC BY 4.0)".

### UCI German Credit

- **What:** 1,000 historical credit applicants with 20 attributes (loan duration, amount, purpose, savings, employment...) and a good/bad credit label.
- **Download:** <https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/144/statlog+german+credit+data>
- **Terms:** CC BY 4.0. Citation: Hofmann, H. (1994). Statlog (German Credit Data) [Dataset]. UCI Machine Learning Repository. <https://doi.org/10.24432/C5NC77>

### UCI Default of Credit Card Clients

- **What:** 30,000 credit card holders in Taiwan (2005) with credit limit, demographics, six months of repayment status, bill and payment amounts, and a default flag.
- **Download:** <https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/350/default+of+credit+card+clients>
- **Terms:** CC BY 4.0. Citation: Yeh, I. (2009). Default of Credit Card Clients [Dataset]. UCI Machine Learning Repository. <https://doi.org/10.24432/C55S3H>

### CFPB Consumer Complaint Database

- **What:** millions of complaints about financial products and services (product, sub-product, issue, company, state, dates, company response, timeliness, and consumer narratives where consent was given, with personal information scrubbed).
- **Download:** <https://files.consumerfinance.gov/ccdb/complaints.csv.zip> (large: several GB unzipped; use a filtered subset). Overview: <https://www.consumerfinance.gov/data-research/consumer-complaints/>
- **Terms:** a work of the US federal government, so in the public domain in the United States under 17 U.S.C. § 105. The CFPB publishes caveats about how the data should be interpreted (it is not a statistical sample); read them before drawing conclusions.

### OFAC sanctions lists

- **What:** the Specially Designated Nationals (SDN) list and consolidated non-SDN lists: names, aliases, addresses, programmes, identifiers.
- **Download:** [OFAC Sanctions List Service](https://ofac.treasury.gov/sanctions-list-service), e.g. `https://sanctionslistservice.ofac.treas.gov/api/PublicationPreview/exports/SDN.CSV` (plus `ADD.CSV`, `ALT.CSV`) or the XML formats.
- **Terms:** US federal government work, public domain in the US (17 U.S.C. § 105).

### UK Sanctions List

- **What:** all UK sanctions designations. Since 28 January 2026 this is the **only** UK source (the OFSI Consolidated List was closed and is kept for reference only).
- **Download:** <https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-uk-sanctions-list> (CSV, XML, ODS)
- **Terms:** Open Government Licence v3.0. Attribution: "Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0."

### PaySim

- **What:** 6.3 million synthetic mobile-money transactions (types CASH-IN, CASH-OUT, DEBIT, PAYMENT, TRANSFER) simulated from real transaction patterns of an African mobile-money service, with fraud labels.
- **Download:** <https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/ealaxi/paysim1> (free Kaggle account required; or `kaggle datasets download -d ealaxi/paysim1`).
- **Terms:** CC BY-SA 4.0. Credit: E. A. Lopez-Rojas, A. Elmir and S. Axelsson, "PaySim: A financial mobile money simulator for fraud detection", EMSS 2016. If you publish a modified version of the data, publish it under CC BY-SA 4.0 too. Your *code* is not affected.

### IBM AML

- **What:** synthetic transactions from the AMLworld generator with laundering labels and typologies (fan-in, fan-out, cycles, scatter-gather...), in several sizes (HI/LI = high/low illicit ratio; Small/Medium/Large).
- **Download:** <https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/ealtman2019/ibm-transactions-for-anti-money-laundering-aml> (free Kaggle account required). Use `HI-Small_Trans.csv` (~5 million rows) for Phase 1.
- **Terms:** [Community Data License Agreement – Sharing 1.0](https://cdla.io/sharing-1-0/): use, modify and share, including commercially; if you share the data (or modified data), share it under the same agreement.

### GLEIF LEI

- **What:** every Legal Entity Identifier record: legal name, jurisdiction, legal form, registered and headquarters addresses, status, and parent relationships. Over 2.5 million entities, updated three times a day.
- **Download:** [GLEIF Golden Copy](https://www.gleif.org/en/lei-data/gleif-golden-copy) (CSV/JSON/XML, full and delta files), or the free API `https://api.gleif.org/api/v1/lei-records?filter[entity.legalAddress.country]=NG`.
- **Terms:** [GLEIF data terms of use](https://www.gleif.org/en/meta/lei-data-terms-of-use): CC0 1.0.

## Sources considered and not used

- **ISO 4217 currency lists from third-party mirrors**: the mirror's own compilation is public domain, but the underlying ISO lists have no clear reuse licence. `java.util.Currency` provides the same codes.
- **OpenSanctions**: excellent aggregated data, but commercial use requires a paid licence.
